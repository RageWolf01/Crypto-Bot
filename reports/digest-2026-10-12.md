## Sharia screening

> There is **no scholarly consensus** on cryptocurrency, and AAOIFI has issued **no final standard** on it. This section reports what named scholars and bodies have published and applies transparent rules. It does not rule, and it is not a fatwa.

### Applies to every asset, regardless of which coin

These are the findings that matter most, and they concern *how* you use crypto rather than *which* coin you hold:

**Leverage, margin and futures** (`UN-001`, gharar, fatal)

The prohibition here is essentially universal across schools and applies regardless of which coin is involved. You cannot sell what you do not own, and a contract whose settlement depends on a future price is gharar.

**Guaranteed or fixed 'earn' yield** (`UN-002`, riba, fatal)

Any product advertising a fixed or guaranteed return on a deposit is the definition of riba al-fadl / riba al-nasi'ah, whatever the asset it is denominated in.

**Day-trading on noise with no productive intent** (`UN-003`, maysir, minor)

Repeated short-horizon speculation is treated by many scholars as closer to gambling than investing, even when the underlying asset is permissible. Intent and holding period matter.

### Where the authorities stand

There is no scholarly consensus on whether cryptocurrency is permissible, and AAOIFI has issued no final standard on it. Three broad positions are held by serious authorities.

- **Mufti Faraz Adam (Amanah Advisors); Shariah Review Bureau (Bahrain)** (permissible): Crypto assets can satisfy the conditions of mal and are acceptable as a medium of exchange, so spot ownership is permissible subject to the ordinary prohibitions on riba, gharar and maysir. [source](https://amanahadvisors.com/), [source](https://www.srbh.org/)
- **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyyah (Grand Mufti Shawki Allam); Diyanet (Türkiye)** (impermissible): Dealing in Bitcoin is prohibited: the gharar is excessive, there is no recognised issuing authority standing behind the value, and the asset facilitates speculation and illicit use. [source](https://www.dar-alifta.org/), [source](https://www.diyanet.gov.tr/)
- **Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani; International Islamic Fiqh Academy (OIC)** (conditional): Cautious. Mufti Taqi Usmani is reported to consider trading impermissible in its current speculative form but to leave the ruling open to revision if crypto becomes tied to real economic activity. The IIFA's 2019 resolution (237) called for further research and its 2021 symposium deferred its recommendations, so the Academy has not closed the question. [source](https://halalscreener.app/en/answers/aaoifi-cryptocurrency-ruling), [source](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/resolutions), [source](https://iifa-aifi.org/en/28253.html)
- **Office of Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali al-Sistani** (context_dependent): Withholds a ruling on buying and selling virtual currencies and directs followers to the next most learned jurist. Also holds that local law regulating crypto must be respected. [source](https://www.sistani.org/)
- **AAOIFI (as an institution)** (conditional): Has not issued a final Shariah standard on cryptocurrency. Its Standard No. 21 quantitative screens (interest-bearing debt and securities under 30% of market cap; non-permissible income under 5% of revenue) are designed for equities and are applied to coins only by analogy. [source](https://aaoifi.com/shariaa-standards/), [source](https://aaoifi.com/ss-21-financial-papers-shares-and-bonds/?lang=en)

## Method

### Data sources

- **CoinPaprika** - market caps, ranks and prices. Chosen because it returns the whole market in a single unmetered call, where CoinGecko's free tier rate-limits after a handful of requests.
- **Binance** - OHLCV history, live order books and 24h tickers. The exchange is the only source of what liquidity actually looks like, as opposed to what a market-cap aggregator reports.
- **DefiLlama** - protocol revenue, TVL, categories and stablecoin data. The `category` field is the independent classification the Sharia sector screen depends on, precisely because it is not written by the projects.
- **alternative.me** - the Fear & Greed index, used only for regime sizing.

### The six factor groups

| Group | Weight | What it measures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| liquidity | 25% | Order-book depth, spread and turnover. Depth is measured within a 2% price band, because slippage is what you actually pay. |
| momentum | 20% | Risk-adjusted returns across 30/90/180-day windows, z-scored against the peer set and clipped so one outlier cannot dominate. |
| tokenomics | 20% | Dilution, float and supply schedule. FDV versus market cap is the most-missed risk in token investing. |
| traction | 20% | Whether the protocol earns real revenue, and how that revenue is trending. |
| valuation | 15% | Revenue multiple and revenue yield, against the peer set. |

Regime is deliberately **not** a factor. Market conditions should change how much you hold, not which asset you pick, and blending the two produces a score that rises in a bull market for reasons unrelated to the asset.

### Hard disqualifiers

| Gate | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Median daily volume | under $5.00M |
| Market cap | under $100.00M |
| Order-book depth | under $250.00K |
| Bid-ask spread | over 25 bps |
| Listing age | under 365 days |
| Stablecoins | excluded |

### Known limitations, stated up front

- **Survivorship bias.** Market-cap aggregators list coins that survived. The thousands that went to zero are largely absent, so the universe is optimistically selected by construction.
- **Volume can be faked.** Wash trading inflates reported volume on thinly supervised venues. The tool uses median rather than mean volume, measures order-book depth directly on Binance, and treats turnover far above 1.0 on a small cap as a warning rather than a strength.
- **TVL is gameable and double-counted.** Deposits can be recycled across protocols and counted twice. It is reported, but it is not the traction measure that matters; revenue is.
- **No forward signal exists.** Nothing here predicts returns. The factors measure the quality and tradability of what is already visible.
- **Overfitting is the default failure mode** of this kind of tool. The weights in `config.toml` are judgement calls, not fitted parameters, and should be treated as such.
- **Professionals lose to the market anyway.** A tool that ranks coins does not change the difficulty of picking winners, which is why the report emphasises risk and position sizing over selection.
